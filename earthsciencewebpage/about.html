My Ties to South Africa

I was born and raised in Gauteng, South Africa. I lived there for almost 20 years with my family who has been there for generations. My mother's side, the Hugo's and Armstrong's were among the first of the Dutch to settle in South Africa.

An Interesting Feature of South Africa's Physical Geography

The Great Escarpment and Drakensberg (Dragon's Mountain) spans over 1100 kilometers. A large portion of South Africa averages 1500m above sea level, known as the Highveld.

How the Earth Testifies of a Creator

"All things denote there is a God; yea, even the earth, and all things that are upon the face of it, yea, and its motion, yea, and also all the planets which move in their regular form do witness that there is a Supreme Creator." — Alma 30:44

Just how Alma notes that the regular motion of the earth and planets testifies of a Creator, we can also look at the intricate and beautiful design of the earth itself testifies of the same. There is far too much detail, intricacy, and beauty in the landscape to believe that it was simply a cosmic accident that it ended up this way. The perfect temperatures for life to thrive, the variety in seasons and geography, the oceans, skies, land housing countless self-sustaining ecosystems. It all testifies that someone who cares put this all together.
